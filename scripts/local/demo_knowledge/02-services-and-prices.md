# Maple Lane Dental: services and prices

All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD) before insurance. Maple Lane Dental is a
fictional business made up for the Luciel demo.

## Check-ups and cleanings
- New patient exam, cleaning and x-rays: $210
- Routine check-up and cleaning (existing patients): $145
- Children's check-up and cleaning (under 12): $95
- We recommend a check-up and cleaning every six months.

## Cosmetic dentistry
- In-office teeth whitening (one 90-minute visit): $450
- Take-home whitening trays with gel: $295
- Whitening is not recommended during pregnancy.

## Fillings, crowns and root canals
- Tooth-coloured (composite) filling: from $160 per tooth
- Porcelain crown: from $1,250 per tooth, with a free consultation first
- Root canal treatment on a front tooth: from $850
- Night guard for teeth grinding: $395

## Emergency visits
- Emergency exam: $120 (treatment is quoted during the visit)
- We keep time for same-day emergency visits Monday to Friday. Call (416) 555-0142 as
  early in the day as you can.
- After hours, for severe swelling, bleeding that will not stop, or trouble breathing
  or swallowing, go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

## Services we do not offer
We do not place dental implants, do orthodontics (braces or clear aligners) or remove
wisdom teeth under sedation. We refer patients to trusted specialists for these.
