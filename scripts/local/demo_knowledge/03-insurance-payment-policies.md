# Maple Lane Dental: insurance, payment and clinic policies

Maple Lane Dental is a fictional business made up for the Luciel demo.

## Insurance
- We bill most major Canadian dental insurance plans directly, so you usually pay only
  the part your plan does not cover.
- Bring your insurance card to your first visit.
- We can send a pre-treatment estimate to your insurer before larger treatments such as
  crowns or root canals.

## Payment
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, debit and e-transfer. We do not accept cheques.
- Payment is due at the end of each visit.
- Payment plans are available for treatment plans over $1,000: up to six monthly
  payments with no interest.

## Cancellations and missed appointments
- Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment.
- Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed, are charged a
  $50 fee. The fee is waived once a year and for emergencies.

## Children and anxious patients
- Children are welcome from their first birthday. A parent or guardian stays with
  children under 16 during the visit.
- For anxious patients we offer longer appointments, noise-cancelling headphones and
  nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for $75 per visit.

## Privacy
Your health records are kept confidential and are never shared without your written
consent, except with your insurer for billing when you ask us to bill them directly.
