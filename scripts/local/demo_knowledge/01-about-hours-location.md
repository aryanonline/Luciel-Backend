# Maple Lane Dental: about us, opening hours and how to find us

Maple Lane Dental is a family dental clinic in Toronto, Ontario. Note: Maple Lane Dental
is a fictional business made up for the Luciel demo.

## Our team
- Dr. Priya Nair, DDS, general and family dentistry (owner since 2014).
- Dr. Daniel Okafor, DDS, restorative dentistry, crowns and emergency visits.
- Two registered dental hygienists and a front-desk team of three.

New patients of all ages are welcome, including children. We speak English, French,
Hindi and Yoruba.

## Opening hours
- Monday to Thursday: 8:00 am to 6:00 pm
- Friday: 8:00 am to 4:00 pm
- Saturday: 9:00 am to 2:00 pm (cleanings and check-ups only, no emergency visits)
- Sunday: closed
- Statutory holidays: closed

## Location and parking
- Address: 88 Maple Lane, Suite 200, Toronto, ON
- Phone: (416) 555-0142
- Email: hello@maplelane-dental.example
- Free parking for patients in the lot behind the building (enter from Birch Street).
- The 504 streetcar stops at Maple Lane, a two-minute walk away.
- The clinic is on the second floor; there is an elevator and the clinic is wheelchair
  accessible.

## Booking an appointment
Call (416) 555-0142 during opening hours or email hello@maplelane-dental.example with
your name, phone number and two times that suit you. New patients should arrive 10
minutes early to fill in a health history form. We usually have check-up appointments
available within one to two weeks.
